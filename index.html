Stewarding family legacy across generations and borders

AFOLI is a not-for-profit institute that supports leadership, governance and next-generation development for Asian family offices and the families they serve.

Get involved Learn more

Our Mission

Education and dialogue for the people who carry family enterprises forward

Family offices across Asia and the diaspora are managing a historic transfer of wealth, responsibility and values to a new generation. Many of the questions they face are not only financial. They are about governance, succession, purpose and how to work across cultures and jurisdictions.

AFOLI brings principals, next-generation family members, advisors and educators together to learn from one another, share good practice and build the relationships that make long-term stewardship possible.

Our Work

Four areas of focus

01

Leadership & Governance

Programs on family governance, decision-making and the role of the family office as an institution that outlasts any one generation.

02

Next-Generation Development

Workshops and peer forums that prepare rising family members to take on leadership, stewardship and philanthropic roles with confidence.

03

Cross-Border Dialogue

Convenings that connect families and practitioners across Asia, North America and Europe to compare perspectives and build trusted networks.

04

Research & Knowledge Sharing

Insights, case discussions and resources on the practices that help family enterprises and family offices endure.

Leadership

Board of Directors

Shirley Hon

Director

Read bio

Shirley Hon is the founder of GloboConsult, a New York-based independent advisory firm serving Asian family offices, and has spent more than a decade in the family office field.

She began that work as chief of staff at a leading Hong Kong family office and later served as chief of staff for a Southeast Asian family office establishing its presence on Wall Street. Earlier, she built the China platform of the United Nations Global Compact as head of external relations.

Shirley speaks on family governance and cross-border development for Asian families at venues including the Harvard Club of New York, NYU and Cornell University.

Jonathan Hsu

Co-Founder & Director

Read bio

Jonathan Hsu is a co-founder of AFOLI, with decades of experience spanning executive leadership, investor relations, international business development and global event management.

He previously held executive leadership roles with Shin Kong Financial Holdings and IMM International, the Taipei-headquartered organization behind the International Dragon Award, and played a key role in establishing and expanding the Registered Financial Consultant (RFC®) designation across Taiwan, Mainland China, Hong Kong and Macau.

Through AFOLI, Jonathan is committed to building enduring relationships across borders and creating platforms that foster meaningful long-term collaboration.
