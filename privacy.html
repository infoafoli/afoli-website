Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

The Asian Family Office Leadership Institute, Inc. ("AFOLI", "we") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through www.afoli.org and how we use it.

Information we collect

We only collect the information you choose to send us by email, such as your name, email address, organization and the content of your message.

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies on this website.

How we use it

  • To respond to your enquiry.
  • To send you occasional AFOLI updates, if you ask to receive them. You can unsubscribe at any time by emailing us.
  • To keep a record of our correspondence.

We do not sell or rent your personal information.

Service providers

Our email is hosted by Google Workspace. The website is hosted by GitHub Pages and uses Google Fonts. These providers may process technical information such as your IP address in order to deliver their services.

Your choices

You may ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to stop sending you updates, by emailing info@afoli.org.
